# Stacks Miners

Stacks miners are the source of the BTC reward pool. They spend BTC to compete for STX block rewards and transaction fees, and those [BTC bids](../glossary.md#miner-bids) fund the [waterfall](../glossary.md#waterfall-yield-distribution) distribution across [paired BTC](../glossary.md#paired-btc-and-paired-stx) participants, the [reserve fund](../glossary.md#reserve-fund-tranche-3), and [STX-only stakers](../glossary.md#stx-only-staking-tranche-2-path).
