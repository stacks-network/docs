# STX-Only Stakers

[STX-only stakers](../glossary.md#stx-only-staking-tranche-2-path) continue the existing stacking-style participation path without pairing BTC. In [PoX-5](../glossary.md#pox-pox-4-pox-5-pox-6), they receive residual [Tranche 2](../glossary.md#t1-t2-t3-waterfall-tranches) BTC yield after paired BTC obligations are handled, and they must still be associated with a [Stacks signer](../glossary.md#stacks-signer).
