# STX-Only Stakers

[STX-only stakers](../glossary.md#stx-only-staking-tranche-2-path) continue the existing stacking-style participation path without pairing BTC. In [PoX-5](../glossary.md#pox-pox-4-pox-5-pox-6), they receive residual [STX-only staking tranche](../glossary.md#stx-only-staking-tranche-2-path) BTC yield after paired BTC obligations are handled, ahead of the [reserve fund](../glossary.md#reserve-fund-tranche-3) allocation, and they must still be associated with a [Stacks signer](../glossary.md#stacks-signer).
