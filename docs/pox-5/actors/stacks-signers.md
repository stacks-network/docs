# Stacks Signers

[Stacks signers](../glossary.md#stacks-signer) validate blocks under [Nakamoto consensus](../glossary.md#nakamoto-consensus-sip-021), with [signing weight](../glossary.md#signing-weight) determined by locked STX. Every participant, whether [BTC-paired](../glossary.md#paired-btc-and-paired-stx) or [STX-only](../glossary.md#stx-only-staking-tranche-2-path), must be associated with a signer by running one directly or delegating to a signer operator.
