# sBTC Pool Participants

[sBTC pool participants](../glossary.md#sbtc-protocol-bond-via-pool) access the BTC staking tranche through a [whitelisted](../glossary.md#whitelist-and-whitelist-row) pool operator instead of managing a native [Bitcoin L1](../glossary.md#l1-and-l2-commitment-layers) timelock themselves. Their enrollment, lock management, and pro-rata reward distribution happen through the pool, and their [sBTC](../glossary.md#sbtc) is locked in the pool contract for the [bond period](../glossary.md#bonding-period).
