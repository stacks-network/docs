# Whitelisted Institutional Partners

[Whitelisted institutional partners](../glossary.md#whitelist-and-whitelist-row) receive negotiated BTC capacity allocations during the [PoX-5 bootstrap](../glossary.md#bootstrap-phase-pox-5). They integrate custody workflows, construct [L1 timelocks](../glossary.md#p2wsh-cltv-l1-timelock-script), lock [paired STX](../glossary.md#paired-btc-and-paired-stx), and expose bond lifecycle state to their users or internal operators.
